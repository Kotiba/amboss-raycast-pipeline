## Summary

Metabolic syndrome is the co-occurrence of medical conditions that increase the risk for <u>atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease</u> (<u>ASCVD</u>) and type 2 <u>diabetes</u>: elevated blood <u>glucose</u>, <u>hypertension</u>, <u>dyslipidemia</u>, and/or <u>abdominal obesity</u>. <u>Management of metabolic syndrome</u> includes optimization of cardiovascular health (e.g., <u>lifestyle modifications for ASCVD prevention</u>) and treatment of the individual components of metabolic syndrome (e.g., <u>management of hypertension</u> and <u>treatment of hyperlipidemia</u>). Screening for the individual components of metabolic syndrome is recommended; the frequency and nature of screening varies based on <u>risk factors</u>. Cardiovascular-<u>kidney</u>-metabolic (CKM) syndrome is an emerging concept developed by the American <u>Heart</u> Association to describe the interaction between metabolic <u>risk factors</u> (e.g., <u>obesity</u>, <u>diabetes</u>, <u>hypertension</u>, <u>hypertriglyceridemia</u>), <u>chronic kidney disease</u> (<u>CKD</u>), and cardiovascular disease (<u>CVD</u>). Adverse <u>social determinants of health</u> (<u>SDOH</u>) are known to increase the risk for both metabolic syndrome and <u>CKM syndrome</u> and should be addressed as part of management.

## Epidemiology

The <u>prevalence</u> of metabolic syndrome is increased in: [[1]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/R6dlkI0)




* Individuals with certain adverse <u>SDOH</u> (e.g., lower income families)

* Older adults compared to younger adults

## Pathophysiology

Dysfunctional <u>adipose tissue</u> → inflammation and <u>insulin resistance</u> → abnormal lipid and <u>glucose metabolism</u> [[2]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/n6d7lI0)




> [!TIP]
> <u>Abdominal obesity</u> (i.e., accumulation of fat in <u>visceral</u> tissue) is strongly associated with an atherogenic and <u>hyperglycemic</u> state. [[2]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/n6d7lI0)[[3]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/Z3aZS4)
