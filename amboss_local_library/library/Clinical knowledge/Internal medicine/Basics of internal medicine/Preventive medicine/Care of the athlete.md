## Summary

<u>Regular physical activity</u> is recommended for people of all ages and should be tailored to each person’s health status, abilities, and goals. For school- and college-aged individuals involved in sports, a pre-participation physical evaluation may be performed to support safe participation by identifying conditions that could increase the risk of injury or other health complications. Athletes of all ages should be counseled on wellness topics, including adequate nutrition, implications of supplement and <u>performance-enhancing drug</u> use, and injury-prevention strategies. While regular exercise offers significant physical and mental health benefits, improper training or excessive exercise may lead to conditions such as overuse injuries and performance-related <u>anxiety</u>. Early recognition and management of exercise-related conditions are critical to maintaining safe, ongoing participation in physical activity.

## Relative energy deficiency in sport

### Definitions [[15]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/gqdFBI0)




* <u>Relative energy deficiency in sport</u> (<u>REDs</u>) 

* A constellation of symptoms resulting from physiological and/or psychological dysfunction in individuals with inadequate energy intake relative to <u>energy expenditure</u>

* Can affect both male and female athletes

* <u>Female athlete triad</u> [[16]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/e-dxws0)

* A term traditionally used to describe certain features of <u>REDs</u>

* A constellation of clinical findings in <u>adolescent</u> and young adult female athletes that comprises: 

* Relative calorie deficit compared to energy needs, with or without an <u>eating disorder</u>

* Menstrual dysfunction

* Decreased <u>bone density</u>





> [!TIP]
> Unlike the <u>female athlete triad</u>, <u>REDs</u> can occur in both men and women, and encompasses a wider range of adverse health effects due to an imbalance in energy intake and expenditure. [[17]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/uzdpus0)




### Clinical features [[15]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/gqdFBI0)




* General

* Impaired athletic performance

* Impaired neurocognitive function

* Increased susceptibility to infection

* Hunger

* Endocrine

* <u>Abnormal pediatric growth</u> patterns

* <u>Amenorrhea</u> or <u>oligomenorrhea</u>

* <u>Clinical features of male hypogonadism</u>

* Psychological

* <u>Symptoms of depression</u>

* <u>Clinical features of eating disorders</u>

* Gastrointestinal

* Change in bowel habits

* Abdominal <u>pain</u>, <u>bloating</u>

* Musculoskeletal

* <u>Clinical features of stress fractures</u>

* Increased susceptibility to injury





> [!TIP]
> <u>REDs</u> is more common in female individuals than in male individuals. [[15]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/gqdFBI0)




### Diagnosis [[15]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/gqdFBI0)




* Diagnosis is made clinically based on history and <u>physical examination</u>.

* Laboratory and imaging studies may be indicated to exclude alternative diagnoses and support the diagnosis. [[15]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/gqdFBI0)[[18]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/YwdnhH0)

* <u>Diagnostics for amenorrhea</u>: Findings in <u>REDs</u> are consistent with <u>functional hypothalamic amenorrhea</u>.

* <u>Diagnostics for male hypogonadism</u>: ↓ <u>testosterone</u> (free or total)

* <u>CMP</u>: <u>Electrolyte</u> abnormalities may be present.

* <u>Diagnostics for iron deficiency</u>: to exclude <u>iron deficiency anemia</u> as a cause of symptoms

* <u>Lipid panel</u>: may show <u>hypercholesterolemia</u>

* <u>DEXA scan</u>: ↓ <u>bone mineral density</u>





> [!TIP]
> Use of <u>oral contraceptives</u> can mask the presence of menstrual abnormalities. [[2]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/Nw1-QQ0)




### Management [[15]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/gqdFBI0)[[17]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/uzdpus0)[[18]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/YwdnhH0)




Management involves a multidisciplinary team with the goal of balancing energy intake with expenditure.




* Refer to a nutritionist for dietary counseling.

* Evaluate the need for changes to sports participation.

* Severe manifestations of <u>REDs</u> (e.g., <u>red flags in eating disorders</u>): Removal from participation and urgent medical evaluation are required.

* Multiple manifestations of <u>REDs</u>: Modifications to training or removal from participation may be necessary.

* Provide management of specific disease manifestations, e.g.:

* <u>Psychotherapy</u> and/or pharmacotherapy for mental health conditions

* Treatment of <u>eating disorders</u> (see “<u>Evaluation of suspected eating disorder</u>”)

* See also “<u>Management of functional hypothalamic amenorrhea</u>.”





### Prevention [[15]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/gqdFBI0)[[18]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/YwdnhH0)




* Educate athletes on the importance of balancing energy intake with activity levels.

* Screen athletes for features of <u>REDs</u> (e.g., during pre-participation physical examinations).
