# Adult health maintenance

[Original Article Link](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/580iM3)

## Summary

Adult health maintenance focuses on the prevention and early detection of disease and is usually addressed during routine physical examinations and <u>Medicare</u> Wellness Visits (for <u>Medicare Part B</u> beneficiaries), but can also occur at other office visits and outside of a clinical encounter. Important components of adult health maintenance include identifying <u>risk factors</u> for disease, providing <u>recommended vaccinations</u>, counseling on health behaviors, and performing <u>screening studies</u>. The recommended <u>screening studies</u> outlined in this article are in line with <u>USPSTF</u> guidance and are based on factors such as age, sex, <u>risk factors</u> for disease, and prior screening history.




See “<u>Well-child visits</u>” and “<u>Adolescent health</u>” for recommendations on routine wellness visits for younger individuals and “<u>Prenatal care</u>” for routine visits in <u>pregnancy</u>. See “<u>Preventive health care for transgender individuals</u>” for special considerations for <u>transgender</u> individuals.

## Screening

* Routine screening allows for the early detection of disease processes and unhealthy behavior.

* Conditions that are screened for include:

* Mental illness, including unhealthy substance use

* <u>Malignancy</u>

* Cardiovascular disease (e.g., <u>hypertension</u>, <u>hyperlipidemia</u>)

* Infectious diseases, including <u>screening for STIs</u>

* Endocrine disorders (e.g., <u>diabetes mellitus</u>, <u>osteoporosis</u>)

* Some <u>screening studies</u> (e.g., <u>colorectal cancer screening</u>) are recommended for all adults, while others (e.g., <u>syphilis screening</u>) are only recommended for those with <u>risk factors</u>.

* For additional <u>screening recommendations</u> in special patient groups, see:

* <u>Screening for geriatric syndromes</u>

* <u>Prenatal care</u>

* <u>Preventive health care for transgender individuals</u>
