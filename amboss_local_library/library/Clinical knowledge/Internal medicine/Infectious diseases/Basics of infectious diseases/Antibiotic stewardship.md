## Summary

<u>Antibiotic</u> stewardship is a coordinated effort to evaluate and improve the prescribing of <u>antibiotics</u> by implementing measures that encourage the proper selection and use of <u>antibiotic</u> regimens. The development of <u>antibiotic</u> resistance with risks of creating <u>multidrug-resistant organisms</u> is one of the largest <u>global</u> and <u>public health</u> threats and can lead to increased <u>morbidity</u> and mortality. <u>Antibiotic</u> stewardship programs (ASPs) are composed of infectious disease consultants, pharmacists, microbiology laboratory staff, and nurses who develop and implement strategies to minimize the harm that can result from <u>antibiotic</u> use while also maximizing the benefits of <u>antibiotic</u> treatment. These strategies may include educational resources; surveillance, monitoring, and reporting of various indicators of adequate <u>antibiotic</u> usage (e.g., prescription rates, <u>antibiotic</u> resistance trends, <u>adverse events</u>); and <u>antibiotic</u> audits and feedback. Expected outcomes of ASPs include reduction in <u>antibiotic</u> resistance rates and improvement of <u>clinical outcomes</u> (e.g., reduction in <u>adverse event</u> rates).
