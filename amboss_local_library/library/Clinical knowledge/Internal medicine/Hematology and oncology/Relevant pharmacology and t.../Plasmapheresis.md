## Summary

Plasmapheresis is an extracorporeal process in which blood is removed from the body and then separated into plasma and blood cells. The blood cells are then returned, and depending on the procedure, the plasma may be returned after purification (selective plasmapheresis) or replaced with donor plasma or <u>albumin</u> solution (nonselective plasmapheresis). Some of the techniques used for purification include immunoadsorption or filtration to selectively remove certain pathogenic blood components (e.g. <u>autoantibodies</u>). Plasmapheresis is used for collecting plasma donations or therapeutically, including for autoimmune (e.g., <u>myasthenia gravis</u>, <u>Goodpasture syndrome</u>, <u>Guillain-Barré syndrome</u>), hematologic (e.g., <u>thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura</u>), metabolic (e.g., severe <u>Wilson disease</u>), and oncologic conditions (e.g., <u>Waldenstrom macroglobulinemia</u>) as well as intoxications (e.g., <u>mushroom poisoning</u>).

## Indications

* Plasma donation

* As a first-line therapy [[2]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/NZc-ba0)

* <u>Guillain-Barré syndrome</u>

* <u>Goodpasture syndrome</u>

* <u>Antiphospholipid syndrome</u>

* <u>Myasthenia gravis</u>

* <u>Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura</u>

* <u>Waldenstrom macroglobulinemia</u>

* Fulminant <u>Wilson disease</u>

* As a second-line therapy [[2]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/NZc-ba0)

* <u>Thyroid storm</u>

* <u>Multiple sclerosis</u>

* <u>Acute disseminated encephalomyelitis</u>

* Severe <u>cold agglutinin disease</u>

* <u>Myeloma cast nephropathy</u>

* <u>Mushroom poisoning</u>
