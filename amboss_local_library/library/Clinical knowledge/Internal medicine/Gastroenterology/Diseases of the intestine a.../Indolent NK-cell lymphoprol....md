## Summary

Indolent natural killer (NK)-cell <u>lymphoproliferative disorder</u> of the <u>gastrointestinal tract</u> (previously known as <u>NK-cell</u> enteropathy) is a benign <u>proliferation</u> of <u>NK cells</u>. The exact cause is unknown, but it may be a localized immune reaction. Patients may be asymptomatic or present with nonspecific gastrointestinal symptoms (e.g., abdominal <u>pain</u>, <u>diarrhea</u>). Diagnosis involves endoscopy with <u>biopsies</u>. The condition is typically indolent, and a watch-and-wait approach is generally recommended.
