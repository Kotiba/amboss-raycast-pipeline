## Summary

<u>Intestinal failure</u> is a condition in which the gut cannot absorb sufficient <u>macronutrients</u> and/or water and <u>electrolytes</u> to maintain health and growth, necessitating intravenous supplementation. The most common cause is <u>short bowel syndrome</u>. Clinical features of <u>intestinal failure</u> vary based on the underlying cause, chronicity, and the resulting degree of metabolic disturbance. Management of chronic <u>intestinal failure</u> focuses on intravenous supplementation and intestinal <u>rehabilitation</u> through diet and pharmacological treatment.
