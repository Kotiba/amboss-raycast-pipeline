## Summary

This article covers various <u>ear</u>, nose, and throat conditions, including <u>external auditory canal atresia</u> and nasal septal <u>ulcers</u>.

## Nasal septal ulceration and perforation

| Overview of <u>nasal septal ulceration</u> and perforation |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | <br>Nasal septal ulceration [[1]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/uvcpbe0)<br> | <br>Nasal septal perforation [[2]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/mrcV3d0)[[3]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/cHca6d0)<br> |
| Definition | <br>* <u>Ulceration</u> through the <u>mucosa</u>, <u>submucosa</u>, and perichondrial layers, resulting in exposure of the nasal <u>cartilage</u><br><br> | <br>* Perforation through all of the layers of the <u>nasal septum</u> (including <u>cartilage</u>), resulting in a communication between the nasal cavities<br><br> |
| Etiology | <br><br>* <u>Iatrogenic</u> (e.g., due to <u>septoplasty</u>, tight nasal packs)<br><br>* Trauma (e.g., nose foreign body, <u>nasal fracture</u>)<br><br>* Substance-related (e.g., from the use of <u>cocaine</u>, intranasal decongestants, intranasal <u>steroids</u>)<br><br>* Autoimmune (e.g., <u>granulomatosis with polyangiitis</u>, <u>systemic lupus erythematosus</u>, <u>sarcoidosis</u>)<br><br>* Infection (e.g., <u>tuberculosis</u>, <u>leprosy</u>, <u>syphilis</u>)<br><br>* <u>Neoplasia</u> (e.g, <u>carcinoma</u> of the <u>nasal septum</u>)<br><br><br> |  |
| Clinical features | <br><br>* Nasal whistling sound (in <u>nasal septal perforation</u>)<br><br>* <u>Epistaxis</u><br><br>* Nasal obstruction<br><br>* <u>Rhinorrhea</u><br><br>* Crusting<br><br>* <u>Hyposmia</u><br><br><br> |  |
| Diagnostics | <br><br>* <u>Anterior rhinoscopy</u> and <u>nasal endoscopy</u><br><br>* Direct visualization of the <u>ulcer</u> and/or perforation<br><br>* Other possible findings: <u>granulation tissue</u>, excessive crusting, foreign body<br><br>* If diagnostics are inconclusive:<br><br>* <u>Laboratory studies</u> (e.g., serological <u>testing for syphilis</u>)<br><br>* <u>Biopsy</u> (e.g., to identify <u>neoplasms</u>, <u>vasculitis</u>)<br><br><br> |  |
| Treatment | <br><br>* General measures: routine saline lavage, application of nasal <u>emollients</u><br><br>* Etiology-directed treatment (e.g., <u>penicillin</u> in patients with <u>syphilis</u>)<br><br><br> | <br><br>* General measures: routine saline lavage, application of nasal <u>emollients</u><br><br>* Etiology-directed treatment (e.g., <u>penicillin</u> in patients with <u>syphilis</u>)<br><br>* Surgical repair<br><br>* Patients who are not suitable for <u>surgery</u> (e.g., inoperable perforation, high risk): nasal septal prosthesis<br><br><br> |
