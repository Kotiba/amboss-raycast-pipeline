## Summary

<u>Nasal turbinate</u> <u>hypertrophy</u> is the enlargement of the <u>nasal turbinates</u>, most commonly the <u>inferior nasal turbinate</u>, and can result in difficulty breathing through the nose. Turbinate <u>hypertrophy</u> is most commonly caused by <u>edema</u> of the nasal <u>mucosa</u> (often due to allergic or <u>vasomotor rhinitis</u>) and/or structural nasal abnormalities. Diagnosis is based on direct visualization of the enlarged turbinates on <u>anterior rhinoscopy</u> or <u>nasal endoscopy</u>. Initial treatment includes intranasal <u>corticosteroids</u> and saline, and treatment of underlying etiologies. <u>Surgery</u> is reserved for refractory cases.
