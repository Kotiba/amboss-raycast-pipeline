## Blood & lymphoreticular system

| Important <u>risk factors</u> for hematological conditions |  |
| --- | --- |
| Condition | <u>Risk factors</u> |
| <u>Burkitt lymphoma</u> | <br><br>* History of <u>infectious mononucleosis</u><br><br>* <u>Immunosuppression</u> (e.g., <u>HIV infection</u>)<br><br><br> |
| <u>Hemolytic anemia</u> | <br><br>* Oxidative <u>stress</u> in individuals with <u>glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency</u><br><br>* <u>Mechanical prosthetic valve</u><br><br><br> |
| <u>Iron deficiency anemia</u> | <br><br>* Female sex<br><br>* <u>Menorrhagia</u><br><br>* <u>Pregnancy</u><br><br>* Diet lacking in <u>iron</u> or <u>vitamin C</u><br><br>* Excessive consumption of cow's milk or <u>caffeine</u><br><br>* <u>H. pylori infection</u><br><br><br> |
| <u>Sickle cell disease</u> | <br><br>* Both parents carry a <u>sickle cell</u> <u>gene</u>.<br><br>* African, Middle Eastern, or Mediterranean descent<br><br><br> |





> [!NOTE]
> 5 Ds mnemonic for <u>risk factors</u> for <u>iron deficiency anemia</u>: Diet (lacking <u>iron</u> and <u>vitamin C</u>, excessive consumption of tea, cow's milk and/or <u>caffeine</u> which blocks <u>iron absorption</u>), Decreased absorption - (due to <u>celiac disease</u>, <u>atrophic gastritis</u>, <u>H. pylori infection</u>), Drugs (<u>aspirin</u>, <u>NSAIDs</u>, <u>PPIs</u>), Donating blood, Drain (<u>GI bleeding</u>, <u>hematuria</u>, <u>menses</u>, <u>pregnancy</u>, lactation).

> [!TIP]
> For both <u>ischemic</u> and <u>hemorrhagic strokes</u>, age (≥ 65 years) is the most important <u>nonmodifiable risk factor</u>, and <u>arterial hypertension</u> is the most important <u>modifiable risk factor</u>.




> [!TIP]
> To remember the causes of <u>carpal tunnel syndrome</u>, think “MEDIAN <u>TRAP</u>”: Myxedema (<u>hypothyroidism</u>), Edema, Diabetes mellitus, Idiopathic, Acromegaly, Neoplasm, Trauma, Rheumatoid <u>arthritis</u>, Amyloidosis, Pregnancy.
