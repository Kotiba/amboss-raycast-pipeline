# Beta-2 adrenergic agonists

## Summary

β<sub>2</sub> adrenergic <u>agonists</u> are used in pulmonology as <u>bronchodilators</u>, in obstetrics as <u>tocolytic agents</u>, and to manage patients with <u>hyperkalemia</u>. There are both short-acting and long-acting β<sub>2</sub> <u>agonists</u>, and their duration of action determines their clinical application. Their effects are achieved through <u>β<sub>2</sub> receptor</u> stimulation. Side effects are partially mediated by the <u>β<sub>1</sub> receptor</u> and include <u>anxiety</u> and <u>tremor</u>, as well as life-threatening conditions such as <u>ventricular arrhythmias</u>.

---

## Pharmacodynamics

* All β<sub>2</sub>adrenergic <u>agonists</u> selectively stimulate β<sub>2</sub>adrenergic <u>receptors</u>.

* Relax bronchial <u>smooth muscle</u>

## Adverse effects

* Cardiac: : ventricular <u>arrhythmias</u>; , <u>vasoconstriction</u>, <u>angina pectoris</u>, <u>tachycardia</u>, and <u>palpitations</u>; may aggravate <u>cardiomyopathy</u> in patients with cardiovascular disease

* <u>Central nervous system</u>/muscular: <u>tremor</u>, <u>headache</u>, <u>anxiety</u>, and <u>sleep</u> disturbances

* <u>Electrolytes</u>

* <u>Hyperglycemia</u>

* <u>Hypokalemia</u> (risk of life-threatening <u>arrhythmias</u>): β<sub>2</sub>-mediated stimulation of <u>Na<sup>+</sup>/K<sup>+</sup>-ATPase</u> → intracellular <u>K<sup>+</sup></u> shift

* Development of tolerance





> [!WARNING]
> Paradoxical <u>bronchospasm</u> may occur!

## Indications

* Bronchial <u>asthma</u>: used to achieve spasmolysis of the <u>bronchi</u>. 

* Acute exacerbation: use short-acting selective β<sub>2</sub>-<u>agonists</u> (e.g., <u>albuterol</u>)

* Prophylaxis (in chronic disease): use long-acting selective β<sub>2</sub>-<u>agonists</u> (e.g., <u>salmeterol</u>)

* <u>COPD</u>: used to achieve spasmolysis of the <u>bronchi</u>.

* Preterm/undesired contractions in obstetrics: used for <u>tocolysis</u>

* <u>Hyperkalemia</u>: Beta-2 adrenergic <u>agonists</u> drive <u>K<sup>+</sup></u> intracellularly.





> [!WARNING]
> <u>Salmeterol</u> should not be used as monotherapy during an <u>acute asthma attack</u>!
