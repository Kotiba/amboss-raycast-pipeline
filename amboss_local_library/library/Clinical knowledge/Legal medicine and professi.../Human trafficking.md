## Summary

Human trafficking is the recruitment, harboring, transportation, or provision of a person for the purpose of <u>labor</u> and/or sexual exploitation through the use of force, fraud, or <u>coercion</u>. <u>Signs of human trafficking</u> are often nonspecific and a high index of suspicion is required to identify affected individuals. Patients may appear fearful and present with injuries inconsistent with their <u>medical history</u> and signs of neglect, abuse, or <u>malnutrition</u>. Management includes treating acute injuries and medical conditions, provision of appropriate local human trafficking resources, and following local laws and institutional guidelines on reporting suspected cases.
