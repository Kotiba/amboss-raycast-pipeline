## Summary

Dysuria is <u>pain</u> or discomfort during <u>urination</u>. It is more common in female than male individuals and can occur at any age. The most common causes of dysuria in female individuals are acute bacterial <u>urinary tract infections</u> (<u>UTIs</u>) and lower genital tract infections (e.g., <u>vaginitis</u>, <u>urethritis</u>) often related to <u>sexually transmitted infections</u> (<u>STIs</u>). In male individuals, bacterial <u>prostatitis</u> and <u>urethritis</u> due to <u>STIs</u> are the most common causes. A focused history, <u>physical examination</u>, and targeted tests, such as <u>urinalysis</u>, help identify the underlying condition and guide further management.
