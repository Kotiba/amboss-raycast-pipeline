## Summary

The <u>USMLE®</u> Content Outline contains exam-related content for all three <u>USMLE</u> examinations (<u>USMLE Step 1</u>, <u>Step 2 CK</u>, and Step 3). Each Step exam focuses on different parts of the outline. Exam candidates are encouraged to review the exam specifications for each exam on the <u>USMLE®</u> website (www.usmle.org) and access the original outline at https://www.usmle.org/sites/default/files/2022-01/USMLE_Content_Outline_0.pdf.
