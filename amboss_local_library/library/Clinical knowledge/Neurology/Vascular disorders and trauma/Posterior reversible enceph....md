## Summary

<u>Posterior</u> reversible <u>encephalopathy</u> syndrome (PRES) is a neurological condition characterized by <u>vasogenic edema</u> predominantly involving the subcortical <u>white matter</u> of the <u>posterior</u> cerebral hemispheres. Causes include <u>acute severe hypertension</u> (e.g., <u>pregnancy-induced hypertension</u>), autoimmune diseases, and certain <u>immunosuppressant</u> or <u>antineoplastic agents</u>. Clinical features typically include <u>altered mental status</u> (<u>AMS</u>), <u>seizures</u>, <u>headache</u>, and/or visual disturbances. Diagnosis relies on <u>neuroimaging</u> (e.g., <u>MRI</u> <u>brain</u>), which typically shows bilateral <u>edema</u> in the parieto-occipital regions. <u>Lumbar puncture</u> may be performed to exclude infectious or inflammatory mimics. Management involves addressing the underlying cause and providing symptomatic support. With early recognition and treatment, most patients achieve complete clinical and radiological recovery.

---

## Etiology

* <u>Acute severe hypertension</u> (e.g., <u>hypertensive emergency</u>, <u>preeclampsia</u>, <u>eclampsia</u>)

* <u>Renal failure</u>

* <u>Sepsis</u>

* Autoimmune diseases (e.g., <u>SLE</u>)

* <u>Antineoplastic drugs</u> and <u>immunosuppressant</u> drugs (e.g., <u>cyclosporine A</u>, <u>cisplatin</u>, <u>cyclophosphamide</u>)

---

## Pathophysiology

The exact pathophysiology of PRES is not fully understood, but the following theories have been proposed. [[3]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/Xyd9ds0)




* ↑ Blood pressure → loss of cerebrovascular autoregulation → hyperperfusion with <u>endothelial</u> damage and <u>vasogenic edema</u>

* Presence of <u>endogenous</u> or <u>exogenous</u> toxins → <u>endothelial</u> dysfunction

## Clinical features

Severity of symptoms may vary. [[2]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/bydHds0)[[3]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/Xyd9ds0)




* <u>Altered mental status</u>

* <u>Seizures</u>

* <u>Headache</u> (typically moderate to severe and generalized)

* Visual disturbances

* <u>Nausea</u>, <u>vomiting</u>
