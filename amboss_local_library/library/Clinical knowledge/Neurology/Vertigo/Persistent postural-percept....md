## Summary

Persistent postural-perceptual <u>dizziness</u> (PPPD), originally known as phobic postural <u>vertigo</u> (PPV), is an <u>idiopathic</u> disorder that presents with a persistent sensation of non-rotational <u>dizziness</u> or <u>unsteadiness</u> and hypersensitivity to motion, lasting at least 3 months. It is the most common cause of chronic <u>dizziness</u> and it often follows an event that caused acute <u>vertigo</u> or <u>dizziness</u>, such as <u>vestibular neuritis</u> or <u>panic attacks</u>. Typically, patients recover from the initial acute disease, but the feeling of <u>dizziness</u> persists. Treatment consists of <u>vestibular rehabilitation</u>, <u>psychotherapy</u> and, if necessary, <u>SSRIs</u>.
