## Summary

Perioral dermatitis is an inflammatory <u>skin</u> condition characterized by <u>erythematous</u> <u>papules</u> and <u>pustules</u> around the mouth that spare the <u>lips</u>. The etiology is unclear, but perioral dermatitis is associated with <u>corticosteroid</u> use and cosmetic products. Diagnosis is usually clinical, although diagnostic testing may be performed to rule out differential diagnoses (e.g., <u>KOH test</u> for <u>fungal infections</u>, <u>patch test</u> for <u>allergic contact dermatitis</u>). Treatment involves cessation of <u>topical corticosteroid</u> and cosmetic use. Topical and oral pharmacotherapy (e.g., <u>antibiotics</u>) may be used in moderate and severe perioral dermatitis.
