## Summary

Chronic <u>liver</u> disease (CLD) is a spectrum of <u>liver</u> diseases that persist for ≥ 6 months. CLD is characterized by progressive <u>liver fibrosis</u>, <u>steatosis</u>, and deterioration of <u>liver function</u>. <u>Cirrhosis</u> is the final stage of CLD. The most common causes of CLD in the US include <u>chronic HCV infection</u>, cardiometabolic <u>risk factors for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease</u> (<u>MASLD</u>), and long-term <u>unhealthy alcohol use</u>. The initial stages of CLD are typically asymptomatic or manifest with nonspecific features (e.g., fatigue, weight loss) before reaching the <u>cirrhosis</u> stage. A prompt workup of CLD is indicated in patients with abnormal <u>liver chemistries</u> and/or imaging findings suggestive of <u>liver</u> pathology (e.g., <u>steatosis</u>, <u>liver</u> <u>nodules</u>) or associated complications (e.g., <u>ascites</u>). Once a cause has been identified, disease-specific evaluation guides management. <u>Liver biopsy</u> is indicated in patients with inconclusive findings on noninvasive diagnostic studies. Complications include <u>acute-on-chronic liver failure</u>, <u>clinically significant portal hypertension</u>, and <u>hepatocellular carcinoma</u>.

## Etiology

* <u>Hepatotoxicity</u>

* <u>Alcohol-associated liver disease</u>

* <u>Drug-induced liver injury</u>;  (e.g., <u>acetaminophen</u>, <u>amiodarone</u>, <u>methotrexate</u>)

* Chronic ingestion of <u>aflatoxin</u> (produced by <u>Aspergillus</u>) [[23]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/bgYHFo)

* Industrial chemicals (e.g., pesticides)




* Inflammation

* Chronic viral infections: <u>HBV</u>, <u>HDV</u>, and <u>HCV</u> (most common cause of <u>cirrhosis</u> in the US)

* <u>Primary biliary cholangitis</u>

* <u>Primary sclerosing cholangitis</u>

* <u>Autoimmune hepatitis</u>

* Parasitic infections (e.g., <u>schistosomiasis</u>, <u>leishmaniasis</u>, <u>malaria</u>)

* <u>IgG4-related sclerosing cholangitis</u>

* Metabolic disorders

* <u>Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease</u>

* <u>Hemochromatosis</u>

* <u>Wilson disease</u>

* <u>α1-antitrypsin deficiency</u>

* <u>Porphyrias</u>

* <u>Glycogen storage disorders</u>

* <u>Cystic fibrosis</u>

* <u>Hereditary fructose intolerance</u>

* <u>Hepatic vein</u> congestion or vascular anomalies

* <u>Budd-Chiari syndrome</u>

* <u>Cardiac cirrhosis</u> (<u>congestive hepatopathy</u>)

* <u>Osler-Weber-Rendu syndrome</u>  [[24]](https://coursology-qbank.com/amboss/article/XgY9Fo)
