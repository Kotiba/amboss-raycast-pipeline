## Summary

<u>Soft tissue</u> injuries of the head and neck are usually caused by blunt or <u>penetrating trauma</u> and require careful clinical assessment. Imaging may be required to rule out <u>fractures</u> and other complications. Initial management of all head and neck injuries follows the Advanced Trauma Life Support (<u>ATLS</u>) protocol and includes addressing life-threatening conditions, such as <u>airway</u> compromise and bleeding. Once initial management has been concluded and complications have been ruled out, open head and <u>neck wounds</u> are managed similarly to other acute <u>open wounds</u>.




For other <u>soft tissue</u> injuries of the neck, see also “<u>Neck sprains</u>,” “<u>Blunt neck injuries</u>,” and “<u>Penetrating neck injuries</u>.”
