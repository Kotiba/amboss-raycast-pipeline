## Summary

Toe deformities are pathological deviations of one or several toes from the anatomical norm. The most common deformity is <u>hallux valgus</u>, a <u>lateral</u> deviation of the large toe at the <u>metatarsophalangeal joint</u>. The etiology of <u>hallux valgus</u> is multifactorial and includes biomechanical instability, poorly fitting shoe wear, neuromuscular diseases, and <u>connective tissue disorders</u>. It presents with <u>pain</u> and inflammation of the <u>metatarsophalangeal joint</u>. Treatment options include special footwear and inlays, <u>pain management</u> and corrective <u>surgery</u>. This article also discusses <u>hallux varus</u>, <u>hammer toe</u>, and <u>claw toe</u>.
